Module 5 · 90 minutes

Limits, risks, and responsibility

Short but non-negotiable. The judgment side of the skill.

What you'll be able to do

Decide what information an agent actually needs to see, put the right checkpoints in front of consequential actions, estimate what a workflow will really cost to run at scale, and recognize when a project needs more than vibecoding alone.

How this module is structured

Four sub-pages, in order:

20 min · Concepts

1. Protect the information

Give the agent the minimum information it needs for the task, not everything you have.
20 min · Concepts

2. Control what the agent can do

Not all actions carry the same risk — put human checkpoints in front of the consequential ones.
20 min · Concepts

3. Understand the real cost

Working is not the same as economical. Model usage, tools, human review, and maintenance all add up.
30 min · Concepts

4. Know when not to vibecode

Capability isn't the same as suitability. When the stakes are high, bring in more than vibecoding alone.
