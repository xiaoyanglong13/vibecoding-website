Business use cases

Real business problems with sample data, a task to try yourself, and a worked solution to compare against. Browse by function.

A finished case starts with a real business task and the data behind it. You try building the solution yourself, verify your output against a checklist, then compare it to a worked solution and see what a good result looks like. A handful of cases below are fully built; the rest are on the roadmap and marked "coming soon." If you've worked through Modules 1–5, you have what you need to follow the live ones.
